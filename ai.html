THE INTELLIGENCE ENGINE

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence is the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines — enabling them to learn, reason, solve problems, perceive, and understand natural language. It is the most transformative technology in human history.


From voice assistants to autonomous vehicles, medical diagnostics to generative art — AI permeates every sector of civilization and accelerates at a pace never seen before.
